Why She Builds Africa

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity isn’t.

We exist to close the gap—equipping African women with the skills, support, and connections they need to build, lead, and shape the future.

We create practical pathways for African women to enter technology, grow with confidence, and turn their potential into influence.

Whether you’re writing your first line of code, changing careers, building a product, or leading a technical team, you should not have to navigate the journey alone.

SkillsPractical, job-ready learning in in-demand tech fields.
MentorshipLearn from experienced women who’ve walked the path.
OpportunityAccess jobs, internships, scholarships, and real-world projects.

Questions

A few things you may want to know

Still unsure? Send us a WhatsApp message and we will help you find the right path.

Who can join She Builds Africa?

African women and young women at any stage, from beginners and students to professionals, founders, mentors, and leaders.

Do I need a technology background?

No. Beginner-friendly activities help you explore the field and build foundations before choosing a focused track.

Are programs online or in person?

Programs can include virtual sessions, in-person workshops, and blended experiences depending on the cohort and city.

How can my organization partner?

You can support training, provide mentors, share roles and scholarships, host events, or sponsor a program.
