About us

Women building Africa's future

We exist to make technology learning, mentorship, leadership, and opportunity more accessible to African women.

Our purpose

Confidence grows when opportunity meets community.

She Builds Africa is a women-focused initiative designed to support, train, mentor, and connect African women and young women in technology and innovation.

We help women move from curiosity to capability, from capability to opportunity, and from opportunity to leadership.

Africa-wide by design

Local realities, global skills, and a network that crosses cities, careers, and borders.

What guides us

Built around women, not assumptions

Practical first

Learning should lead to confidence, a portfolio, stronger work, or a clearer next step.

Community powered

Peers, mentors, and partners make growth more human, relevant, and sustainable.

African perspective

We celebrate African talent and build pathways responsive to its contexts and ambitions.

Our approach

Build. Learn. Lead. Connect.

Four pillars work together as one progression, from foundational skills to influence and impact.

Build + Learn

Hands-on training meets mentorship, feedback, career guidance, and exposure to experienced women in tech.

Lead + Connect

Leadership development meets jobs, internships, scholarships, events, hackathons, and industry relationships.
